About

Ann Rikka (@ann_rikka) is an AI persona project operated by Studio 6 (a Magic Wand Inc. brand). This page covers the privacy practices and terms applicable to the internal Ann Rikka Publisher application used for cross-platform content distribution to Instagram, X, YouTube, and TikTok accounts owned by Studio 6.

Contact: ann.rikka6603@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

1. Scope

The Ann Rikka Publisher application ("the App") is an internal tool used solely by Studio 6 / Magic Wand Inc. for posting pre-produced content to its own social media accounts. The App does not process data of any third-party users.

2. Data Collected

The App processes only the following first-party data:

  • OAuth access tokens issued by Instagram (Meta), X, YouTube (Google), and TikTok for the @ann_rikka, @ann_rikka_, @ann.rikka accounts
  • Pre-produced video, image, caption, and hashtag content created by Studio 6 staff
  • API response metadata returned by the respective platforms (post IDs, status codes, error messages)

The App does NOT:

  • Collect data from end users or viewers of @ann_rikka content
  • Track engagement of third parties
  • Sell, share, or transfer collected data to any external party
  • Use collected data for advertising or profiling

3. Data Storage

All credentials and metadata are stored locally on Studio 6 operator machines, secured by OS-level encryption and access controls. Credentials are never shared, transmitted to third parties, or stored in cloud services.

4. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained only as long as the App is in active use. Tokens are rotated periodically (60-day refresh for IG; 90-day refresh for YouTube). Pre-produced content is retained on operator machines until manual deletion.

5. User Rights

As the App processes no end-user data, there are no third-party user rights to manage. Studio 6 retains full control over its own content and accounts.

6. Children

The App does not target or knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Content posted via the App is intended for general audiences aged 13+.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. Material changes will be noted with a new "Last updated" date.
